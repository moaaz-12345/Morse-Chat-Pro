Morse Chat Pro

Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 16, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Morse Chat Pro collects, uses, stores, and protects information when you use the Morse Chat Pro application and related services.

Information we collect

Depending on how you use the app, we may collect the following information:

  • Account information such as username, email address, phone number, profile photo, status, and bio.
  • Chat content such as messages, Morse text, reactions, pinned messages, and message metadata.
  • Uploaded content such as images, voice messages, and files you choose to send.
  • Call-related metadata such as call type, participants, call status, and call history.
  • Device and technical information needed for authentication, security, app performance, and notifications.

How we use information

We use information to:

  • Create and manage user accounts.
  • Provide private chats, rooms, message history, uploads, reactions, search, and notifications.
  • Support voice and video call functionality.
  • Improve reliability, security, and app performance.
  • Prevent abuse, fraud, unauthorized access, and service misuse.

Messages, uploads, and calls

Messages and uploaded files are processed so the app can deliver them to the intended recipients. Voice and video calls use real-time communication technology. Call media is intended to be transmitted between call participants and is not used for advertising.

Authentication

Morse Chat Pro supports account registration and login. Passwords are protected using secure hashing. If you sign in using a third-party provider such as Google, we may receive basic profile information such as your name, email address, and profile photo as permitted by that provider.

Notifications

The app may use notifications to alert you about messages, calls, and important app activity. You can control notification permissions from your device settings.

Data sharing

We do not sell your personal information. We may share limited information only when necessary to operate the app, comply with legal obligations, protect users, or maintain service security.

Data storage and security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect user information. However, no internet-based service can guarantee absolute security.

Data retention

We keep information for as long as needed to provide the app, maintain security, comply with legal requirements, and support legitimate operational needs.

Your choices

You may update your profile information inside the app. You may also contact us to request help with account or privacy-related questions.

Children

Morse Chat Pro is not specifically directed to children. If you believe a child has provided personal information without appropriate consent, please contact us.

Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Updates will be posted on this page with a new “Last updated” date.
